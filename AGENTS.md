# Portfolio writing rules

Use Tarik’s voice for all portfolio copy and future edits.

- Write in plain, direct, conversational British English.
- Use first person to explain his work: “I designed…”, “I worked with…” and “My role was…”.
- Describe the problem, what he did and why in straightforward language.
- Prefer short sentences and familiar words. Keep technical terms when they explain the work.
- Avoid promotional language, corporate jargon and claims that make the work sound grander than described.
- Correct spelling and grammar while preserving his meaning and natural phrasing.
- Do not invent research, collaboration, leadership activities, outcomes or metrics.
- Keep headings and captions clear and descriptive.

## Publishing

Make and check edits locally. Only push to GitHub or publish a hosted site when Tarik explicitly asks. Local preview uses port 3003.
